# Gochujang-Sesame Sauce

**1 jar · 3 min**

**Used in:** [Wednesday Lunch — Sichuan Crumble Rice Bowl](lunch-wed-sichuan-crumble-bowl.md)

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 clove garlic, grated

## Method

1. Whisk together in the jar you'll store it in. That's it.

## Notes

This one never touches the food processor, which is why it comes second. Whisk it in the jar while the tahini is blitzing.
