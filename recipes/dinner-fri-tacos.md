# Friday — Tacos with Chili-Lime Crumbles

**2 servings · 18 min**

## Ingredients

- Chili-lime tofu crumbles
- 1 can black beans
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 cloves garlic
- Tortillas
- 1/4 cabbage, shredded
- 2 limes
- Quick-pickled onions
- 1 avocado
- Salsa, or fresh pico de gallo
- Cilantro
- Salt

## Method

1. Black beans with cumin and garlic, 5 minutes.
2. Chili-lime crumbles in the same pan after, 4 minutes with a squeeze of lime.
3. Slaw — shredded cabbage, lime, salt. 3 minutes.
4. Tortillas blistered directly over the flame, 4 minutes.
5. Assemble with pickled onions, avocado, salsa, cilantro.

## Notes

18 minutes wall clock, 15 hands-on. Each component is simple, but there are five of them.

TREAT NIGHT: if Impossible grounds or TVP are going to appear in the week, they appear here, and only here. Once a week, maximum.

WITH GARDEN TOMATOES: fresh pico de gallo instead of jarred salsa — tomato, onion, jalapeño, lime, cilantro, salt. Five minutes, and it's not close.

Alternates with falafel night.
