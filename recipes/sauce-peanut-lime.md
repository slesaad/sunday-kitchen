# Peanut-Lime Sauce

**1 jar · 5 min**

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger
- 1-2 tsp chili crisp
- 4-6 tbsp water

## Method

1. Blitz, thinning with water to a pourable consistency.

## Notes

Second in the sauce sequence, straight after the tahini-lemon — no washing needed between them.

For grain bowls, satay tempeh, and anything that wants a Southeast Asian turn.
