# Quick-Pickled Onions

**1 jar · 5 min**

Five minutes of work that makes everything else taste deliberate.

**Used in:** [Tuesday & Thursday Lunch — Mediterranean Bowl](lunch-mediterranean-bowl.md) · [Friday — Tacos with Chili-Lime Crumbles](dinner-fri-tacos.md) · [Friday, alternate — Falafel Night](dinner-fri-falafel-night.md)

## Ingredients

- 1 red onion, sliced thin
- 1/2 cup vinegar, white or apple cider
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp salt

## Method

1. Slice the onion as thin as you can be bothered to — thinner onions pickle faster and taste less raw.
2. Combine everything in a jar.
3. Ready in 30 minutes.

## Notes

Keeps three weeks, and makes leftovers taste deliberate.
