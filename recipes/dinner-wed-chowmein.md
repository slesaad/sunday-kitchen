# Wednesday — Veggie Chowmein

**2 servings · 18 min**

## Ingredients

- Chowmein or lo mein noodles, for 2
- Wednesday stir-fry pack (shredded cabbage, carrot, capsicum, onion)
- 2 eggs, optional
- 1 cup shelled edamame
- 1 cup Sichuan tofu crumbles, optional or instead of the eggs
- 2 cloves garlic
- Oil
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- White pepper
- Chili
- Scallions

## Method

1. Kettle water into a pot. Noodles 3 minutes, drain, toss with a little oil so they don't weld together.
2. Biggest pan you own, as hot as it goes. Oil, garlic, then THE WHOLE STIR-FRY PACK — 5 minutes, hard heat, keep it moving. Crowding steams it; you want char.
3. Push the veg aside and scramble the eggs in the space. Or skip them and add a cup of Sichuan crumbles — the dish doesn't miss them.
4. Noodles and edamame in, then the sauce. Toss hard, 2 minutes.
5. Scallions.

## Notes

18 minutes wall clock, 14 hands-on. The longest dinner of the week, because a stir-fry has to be watched the whole time.

THIS ASSUMES THE STIR-FRY PACK EXISTS. Chopping the vegetables fresh puts this at 28 minutes.

TO GET IT TO 12 MINUTES: cook the noodles on prep day, two minutes under, tossed in oil — that's how restaurants hold lo mein. They're slightly softer by Wednesday, but it removes the boil from the weeknight entirely.
