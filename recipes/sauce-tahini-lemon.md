# Tahini-Lemon Sauce

**1 jar · 5 min**

**Used in:** [Tuesday & Thursday Lunch — Mediterranean Bowl](lunch-mediterranean-bowl.md) · [Friday, alternate — Falafel Night](dinner-fri-falafel-night.md)

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup tahini
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 4-6 tbsp cold water

## Method

1. Blitz everything in the food processor, adding the water last and slowly.
2. IT WILL SEIZE INTO CEMENT BEFORE IT LOOSENS. This alarms everyone the first time. Keep adding water. Keep going. It comes back.

## Notes

Make this FIRST in the sauce sequence — tahini, then gochujang-sesame — and the gochujang never touches the processor, so the bowl gets washed once.
