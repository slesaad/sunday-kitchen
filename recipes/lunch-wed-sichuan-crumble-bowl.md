# Wednesday Lunch — Sichuan Crumble Rice Bowl

**1 servings · 5 min**

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice
- Roasted vegetables
- Shelled edamame
- Cucumber
- Sichuan tofu crumbles, PACKED SEPARATELY
- Gochujang-sesame sauce, PACKED SEPARATELY

## Method

1. Rice, roasted veg, edamame and cucumber in the main container.
2. Sichuan crumbles and gochujang-sesame in two small separate containers.
3. At work: reheat the main container, THEN scatter the crumbles on and add the sauce.

## Notes

Crumbles packed against wet rice lose everything that makes them worth making. The two extra containers are worth the thirty seconds.
