# Friday Lunch — Ragù Rice Bowl

**1 servings · 5 min**

## Ingredients

- Lentil-walnut ragù
- Cooked rice
- Roasted vegetables
- Nutritional yeast, packed separately

## Method

1. Ragù over rice, roasted veg alongside.
2. Nutritional yeast in a separate corner.
3. Packed Wednesday night rather than prep day, so it's two days old rather than five.

## Notes

OVER RICE, NOT PASTA. Reheated pasta in a container goes gluey and sad by Friday. Rice takes exactly the same sauce and holds up for four days.
