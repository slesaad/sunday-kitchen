# The Prep Session

**~2h30 for Week A. ~50 min for Week B.** Two people, two tracks, four heat sources
overlapping.

- **Person 1 — the wet track.** Instant Pot, stovetop, food processor.
- **Person 2 — the dry track.** Knife work, sheet pans, air fryer, containers.

These times include the parts people leave out — waiting for the Instant Pot to come to
pressure, natural release, chopping two trays of vegetables. There's a trim list at the
bottom if you want to get it closer to two hours.

---

## Before: two small things

**Midweek, 30 seconds.** Put four blocks of extra-firm tofu in the freezer, still in their
packages. Freezing restructures tofu into something spongy and chewy that soaks up
marinade and crisps far better — and **you never have to press tofu again.**

**Saturday, 5 minutes.**
- Move the frozen tofu to the fridge to thaw overnight.
- Put 2 cups of dried chickpeas in a bowl with plenty of water to soak.

## Week A — full prep, ~2h30

### 0:00–0:05 — Setup, both of you

Oven to **425°F / 220°C**. Three sheet pans lined with parchment. Everything out of the
fridge and pantry and onto the counter.

### The Instant Pot does two jobs

Chana first, rice second. The chana is the long one, so it goes in early and the rice
slots into the gap while you're portioning. Don't try to do both at once — the pressure
times aren't compatible.

### Person 1 — wet track

| Clock | Do this | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:05** | Instant Pot on Sauté. Oil, cumin seeds, then onions — **a full 10 minutes**, until genuinely golden. Don't rush this one. | 10 min |
| **0:15** | Garlic, ginger, tomato paste, ground spices, 2 min. Then tomatoes, drained chickpeas, water, salt. | 5 min |
| **0:20** | Seal. 18 min high pressure, 10 min natural release. **It takes ~12 min to come to pressure at this volume** — that's why it's done at 1:00, not 0:38. | *hands off → 1:00* |
| **0:22** | Toast the walnuts in a dry pan, 4 min. Pulse the ragù soffritto in the food processor. | 8 min |
| **0:30** | Soffritto into olive oil on the stovetop. **12 minutes** until soft and sweet. Garlic at the end. | 12 min |
| **0:42** | Tomato paste 2 min, then tomatoes, lentils, broth, herbs, fennel seeds. Down to a simmer. | *hands off → 1:20* |
| **0:45** | **Three sauces**, in this order so you never wash the processor bowl: tahini-lemon → peanut-lime → gochujang-sesame (that last one is a whisk in a jar). | 15 min |
| **1:00** | Chana done. Mash a few chickpeas against the pot to thicken. Garam masala and amchur in **off the heat**. Spread on a sheet pan to cool. | 5 min |
| **1:05** | Rinse the Instant Pot. **Rice:** 3 cups rinsed basmati, 3 cups water, 6 min high pressure, 10 min natural release. | *hands off → 1:30* |
| **1:10** | Wash down the wet station. Stir the ragù. | 10 min |
| **1:20** | Ragù done — walnuts in for the last 5 min, salt hard, off the heat. | 3 min |
| **1:30** | Rice out. Fluff, spread on a sheet pan to cool fast. | 3 min |

### Person 2 — dry track

| Clock | Do this | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:05** | **Crumbles.** Crumble 2 blocks of tofu by hand into pea-to-marble pieces. Toss with the base. Spread on one pan in **three zones**, seasoned separately. | 15 min |
| **0:20** | Crumbles into the oven. 25–30 min, stir at 15. | *hands off → 0:50* |
| **0:22** | **Tofu.** Squeeze the thawed blocks hard over the sink. Cube, toss in the marinade. | 8 min |
| **0:30** | **Chop two roasting trays.** Everything the same size. One harissa (plus a drained can of chickpeas), one chili-cumin. This is the longest single job in the session. | 20 min |
| **0:50** | Crumbles out. Both veg trays in. 30 min at 425°F. | *hands off → 1:20* |
| **0:52** | Marinated tofu into the air fryer. 400°F, 18 min, shake at 9. | *hands off → 1:10* |
| **0:55** | **The Wednesday stir-fry pack.** Shred cabbage, carrot, capsicum and onion into one bag. **This is what makes Wednesday a 15-minute dinner instead of a 28-minute one.** | 15 min |
| **1:10** | Tofu out of the air fryer. | 2 min |
| **1:20** | Veg trays out. Quick-pickle a red onion — vinegar, hot water, sugar, salt, jar. | 6 min |
| **1:28** | Wash and spin the greens. Chop the herbs. | 8 min |

### 1:35–2:20 — Both: cool, portion, label

Forty-five minutes, and it's the part everyone underestimates. You're filling roughly
twenty containers.

1. **Freeze half of both anchors** in bags laid **flat** — they stack, and flat bags thaw in
   an hour instead of a day. That's next week's dinner, already done.
2. **Rice: half to the fridge, half frozen flat in portions.** Frozen rice microwaves
   better than four-day-old fridge rice, and it skips the food-safety question entirely.
3. **Pack Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday's lunches now.** Thursday and Friday get packed
   Wednesday night — see [`01-the-plan.md`](01-the-plan.md) for exactly what goes in each.
4. **Crumbles get their own container**, and stay divided by zone. They go soft against wet
   food, and the whole point is that they're three different things.
5. **Sauces in jars, undressed.** Especially the tahini.
6. **Label with the date.** You will not remember.

## Getting it under two hours

Every one of these is a real trim, in the order I'd cut them:

| Cut | Saves |
|---|---|
| **Buy pre-shredded coleslaw mix** instead of chopping the stir-fry pack | 12 min |
| **Buy one bag of pre-cut vegetables** for a roasting tray | 10 min |
| **Make two sauces instead of three** | 8 min |
| **Use canned chickpeas** for the chana — 5 min pressure instead of 18 | 25 min of clock, 0 min of hands-on |

The coleslaw mix is the one I'd actually do. It's the same cabbage and carrot, it's cheap,
and shredding cabbage is the least interesting fifteen minutes of the session.

**Never cut:** the anchors, the crumbles, or the stir-fry pack. Those are the three things
the week depends on.

## Week B — freezer week, ~50 min

Move the frozen anchors to the fridge **Friday night** so they're thawed.

| Clock | Do this |
|---|---|
| **0:00** | Oven to 425°F. Crumble 2 blocks of tofu, toss, three zones, into the oven. |
| **0:15** | Chop one veg tray — **different vegetables and a different spice from last week.** Into the oven. |
| **0:25** | Rice into the Instant Pot. 6 min pressure, 10 min natural release. |
| **0:28** | Shred the stir-fry pack, or open the coleslaw bag. |
| **0:35** | One new sauce — pick one you didn't make last week. |
| **0:40** | Air-fry the tofu. |
| **0:50** | Portion, label, done. |

The anchors are already made. They'll taste better than they did the week you cooked them.
