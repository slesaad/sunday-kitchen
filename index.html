Growing the plan

Weeks 1–2: core system only, no projects. Week 3: the seitan session. Week 5: add tempeh if the Asian grocery stocks it.

The three seitan flavors aren't three new dinners — they rotate into slots that already exist. Char siu alternates with Monday's sticky tofu, chorizo swaps into Friday's tacos, and shawarma turns the Mediterranean lunch bowl into a shawarma bowl, taking it from ~33 g protein to ~50 g. No new shopping, no new sauces. A seitan week is less prep-day work, not more.

Two roasting trays, and they are not interchangeable. Same oven, same 30 minutes, but they have different jobs. Harissa veg has exactly one: the four Mediterranean lunch bowls, Tuesday and Thursday, both of you. It looks like a lot on the tray because it is four whole lunches. Chili-cumin veg is every other “roasted veg” in the week — Monday dinner, Wednesday lunch, Thursday dinner. Store the two in separate, labelled containers, or you get one flavour of roasted veg for five days.

Dinners don't get packed. You're eating them at home, so they just get held back in a labelled tub. Only the eight lunch containers get assembled.

Three

Eight lunch containers

One container is one person's whole lunch, finished. Nothing gets added on the day and nothing gets decided in the morning — you open the fridge, take the one with your initial on it, and leave. Pack Monday through Wednesday on Sunday; pack Thursday on Wednesday night, so nothing you eat is five days old. Friday lunch is eaten out — four days, eight containers.

You do not need measuring cups for this. Fill by layer and let the container's own height do the work: rice to a third, the main to three-quarters, edamame and veg fill the rest. In a 3-cup container that lands on 1 cup rice, 1½ cups main, ½ cup edamame — Person A's portion, hit without measuring anything.

Person B doesn't measure differently — Person B gets a bigger container. Use the larger ones you own for B and the smaller for A, and the same rule gives you both the right portion. If every container you own is the same size, B's extra edamame goes in a side pot.

Label each one with an initial and a day — A · Tue, B · Wed. Small pots of sauce or crumbles get taped to their container or nested inside, so each lunch travels as one object.

Anything crisp or creamy travels separately. Tahini dressed on Sunday turns grains to paste by Tuesday, and crumbles packed against wet rice lose everything that makes them worth making.

The Mediterranean bowls are good cold — no microwave needed.

Where the three crumble zones go

One sheet pan, three zones, roughly two cups of each. Every zone has a job. On falafel weeks, season two zones instead of three — Friday is falafel night, so nothing eats the chili–lime. Split the pan between Sichuan and cumin–garam masala.

The Instant Pot does two jobs: chana first, then rice. Don't run both at once — the pressure times aren't compatible.

Either of you can take either track — they're interchangeable. That's different from Person A / Person B further down, which are fixed by bodyweight and only decide portion sizes.

1:40 — 2:25 · Both of you: cool, portion, label

Forty-five minutes, and it's the part everyone underestimates — you're filling the better part of twenty containers.

  • Freeze half of both anchors in bags laid flat. They stack, and flat bags thaw in an hour instead of a day.
  • Rice: half fridge, half frozen flat. Frozen rice microwaves better than four-day-old fridge rice, and it skips the food-safety question entirely.
  • Pack Monday to Wednesday's lunches now. Thursday's get packed Wednesday night. Nothing for Friday.
  • Crumbles and both veg trays get split now, by the portion counts in “Where every batch goes” — not left as one tub each. Sichuan into 3 (two lunch pots, one chowmein tub), cumin–garam into 2, chili–lime stays as 1. Harissa veg into 4 lunch portions, chili-cumin veg into 4 tubs. Zones and trays kept apart.
  • Label with the date. You will not remember.

Supplements

Only have edamame in pods? They work, but the plan puts edamame into bowls, which pods can't do. 2 cups of pods ≈ 1 cup shelled ≈ 18 g protein — about half the weight is inedible pod. Microwave the bag, cool it, and squeeze the beans into a container; roughly 10 minutes a bag, and the portioning phase is a good moment for it. Longer term, buy shelled and keep the pods as a snack — this plan goes through 8–10 cups of shelled beans a week.

Two labels worth checking. Your tofu should say calcium sulfate — that's what makes it a serious calcium source now that dairy is gone. Your soy milk should be fortified with calcium, B12 and D; unfortified soy milk is just beans and water.

A note on Person A's calories

That target is deliberately not a deficit. At 100–106 lb, "gain muscle and lose fat" is best served by eating at maintenance or slightly above, with high protein and progressive overload.

Eating less at that bodyweight costs lean mass, which moves you away from the goal. The leanness comes from adding muscle, not subtracting food.

Four gaps that actually matter

B12 — no reliable plant source, non-negotiable. Iodine — sea salt has none and most multivitamins skip it; cook with iodized salt. Algal DHA/EPA — flax gives ALA, which converts at a few percent. Vitamin D — especially October to March.

Everything else takes care of itself on a diet this varied.

Iron, without a supplement

Plant iron absorbs poorly alone, but vitamin C multiplies absorption several times over. This plan does it by accident — lemon on the chana, peppers in the roasted veg, lime on the tacos, lemon on Tuesday's curry. Keep it deliberate.

Don't drink tea or coffee with meals. The polyphenols cut absorption substantially. Wait an hour.

Creatine

3–5 g daily, timing irrelevant, no loading phase. Keep taking it.

Creatine comes almost entirely from meat, so vegetarians start with lower muscle stores — and tend to see larger gains from supplementing than omnivores do.

What to actually track

Weigh your protein sources for one week only — after that you'll eyeball 150 g of tofu. Read the weekly average of your morning weight, not the daily number.

Track your lifts. Progressive overload causes the muscle gain; the food permits it. Re-evaluate after 6–8 weeks, and adjust rice and oats before you touch protein.

Ramp the fiber up over two weeks. This plan lands around 45–55 g a day. If you're currently eating half that, start with smaller legume portions and build. Drink more water than you think you need — otherwise the first week is unpleasant in an entirely avoidable way.

Your first prep session will run past 2:45 — you'll be reading while cooking, and you won't know where anything is yet. The second one lands on the clock. If it doesn't, take the trim list rather than rushing the onions.
